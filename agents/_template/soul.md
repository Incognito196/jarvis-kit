Write, in plain sentences, what this skill is and how you want it used. This text is
appended to the system prompt verbatim, so talk to the brain the way you'd brief a
person on their first day:

- what the skill can do
- the exact command or route to use, with an example
- the rules: what to confirm first, what never to touch, how to verify it worked

Keep it short. A page of rules is a page the model has to weigh on every single turn.
